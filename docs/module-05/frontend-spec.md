# Module 05 Frontend Specification

## Technology
React, TypeScript, Material UI, React Router, accessible data grids, responsive layouts, and API clients generated from OpenAPI.

## Shared page capabilities
Every page supports responsive design, search, filtering, sorting, bulk actions where applicable, role-based visibility, audit history access, keyboard navigation, focus states, semantic labels, and WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.

## Pages
- Employee Dashboard: workforce KPIs, expiring credentials, onboarding queue, review due queue, training compliance, schedule conflicts.
- Employee Directory: searchable workforce list with category, status, department, branch, role, specialty, and credential filters.
- Employee Profile: master profile tabs for identity, employment, assignments, credentials, health, performance, documents, assets, and audit history.
- Credential Management: license verification, expiry tracking, council registration, board certification, and failed verification workflow.
- Clinical Privileges: scope of practice, procedure authorizations, surgical privileges, radiation and chemotherapy authorizations.
- Department Assignment: primary, secondary, temporary, acting, cross-branch, project, committee, and clinical team assignments.
- Employment History: lifecycle timeline with approved events and effective dates.
- Training Records: completed, due, overdue, CME, mandatory compliance, and research training records.
- Certification Tracker: certification expiry calendar and renewal workflow.
- Schedule Management: shifts, rosters, on-call, clinic sessions, theatres, wards, emergency coverage, and conflict resolution.
- Availability Calendar: vacation, holidays, training days, conferences, research time, and unavailable blocks.
- Performance Reviews: review cycles, reviewer assignments, outcomes, goals, and due alerts.
- Occupational Health: fitness status, restrictions, confidential notes, and clearance workflow.
- Vaccination Records: vaccine status, boosters, overdue mandatory vaccinations, and compliance reports.
- Research Profile: research interests, publications, studies, certifications, and participation.
- Committee Membership: committees, task forces, roles, terms, and attendance references.
- Asset Assignment: devices, uniforms, access cards, clinical equipment, issue and return tracking.
- Document Repository: versioned HR documents, contracts, verification evidence, approvals, and signatures.
- Employee Search: global advanced search across profile, credential, assignment, and schedule fields.
- Reports: directory, headcount, credentials, licenses, privileges, vacancies, coverage, training, performance, research, turnover, demographics, and occupational health.
- Bulk Import Wizard: validate CSV/XLSX imports, preview errors, map fields, submit asynchronous import, and view results.
- Administrator Dashboard: configuration, policy thresholds, notification rules, reference data, security review, and audit monitoring.

# Module 01 Reports

1. **Registration Summary:** total patients by status with foreign and deceased counts.
2. **Duplicate Workqueue:** open duplicate candidates, score, reasons, age, and assigned MPI analyst.
3. **Merge Audit:** source MRN, target MRN, rationale, approver, completion timestamp, and reversal status.
4. **Temporary Patient Aging:** temporary and anonymous emergency records awaiting completion.
5. **Newborn Completion:** newborns missing permanent names, National ID, insurance, or guardian consent.
6. **Deceased Verification:** patients marked deceased with verifier, timestamp, and downstream notification state.
7. **Biometric Consent Compliance:** fingerprint and face recognition captures with active consent linkage.
8. **Identifier Quality:** missing National ID, Passport for foreign patients, duplicate phone numbers, and expired insurance coverage.
